【 觀察的延伸 】
六年的媒體生涯，相機是我觀察世界的第二雙眼睛。
這裡記錄了我穿梭於文化活動、街頭人文與建築線條之間，
以及那些稍縱即逝的落日風景。 【 The Extension of Sight 】
With 6 years in media, the camera has become my second pair of eyes.
These are the visual fragments captured across cultural events, street landscapes, and architectural geometries,
along with the fleeting golden hours.

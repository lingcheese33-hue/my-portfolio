Facebook & Instagram Zero-to-One Social-Media Operation | 2025

Overview / 項目概述
Led full-cycle social-media build-up and daily operation for Chinese-speaking audiences. Covered content calendar arrangement, bilingual marketing copywriting, visual production, boosted-post campaign execution, KPI tracking and performance insight reporting. Applied Canva-AI for graphic production and generative-AI tools to optimise content workflow. 面向中文受眾，負責帳號 0 到 1 搭建與日常全流程運營，包含內容排期、繁簡雙語營銷文案、視覺物料製作、Facebook 付費貼文推廣、KPI 數據監測與成效洞察報告；使用 Canva-AI 製作視覺，借助生成式 AI 優化內容工作流程。

Key Responsibilities / 主要工作
1. Plan monthly content calendar; draft Simplified & Traditional Chinese marketing captions and promotional content.
2. Create social visuals via Canva; execute Facebook boosted-post campaigns.
3. Monitor audience engagement metrics; compile performance reports and extract actionable business insights.
4. Observe Hong Kong and Mainland social trends to guide content iteration. 1. 策劃月度內容排期，撰寫繁簡中文營銷貼文與宣傳內容。
2. 使用 Canva 製作社交視覺物料，執行 Facebook 貼文付費加熱。
3. 監測用戶互動數據，輸出成效報告，提煉可落地營銷洞察。
4. 跟進香港及內地網絡趨勢，反饋優化內容方向。

Key Outcomes / 項目成果
- Grew to 20k+ followers on Facebook, 15k+ followers on Instagram within one-year timeframe.
- Produced multiple high-reach posts with 100k–1M views.
- Regularly delivered data-driven reports to support internal marketing decision-making. - 一年內實現 Facebook 粉絲 20,000+，Instagram 粉絲 15,000+。
- 產出多篇 10 萬–100 萬級瀏覽量爆款貼文。
- 定期輸出數據報告，為內部營銷決策提供參考。
